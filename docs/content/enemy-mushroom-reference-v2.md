# Mushroom Enemy Visual Reference — V2

Status: Active reference
Date: 2026-09-18

## Why V1 is rejected

The first mushroom family used the same broad cap + cylinder body + oversized oval eyes for every form. The result failed three production requirements:

- the eyes occupied too much of the face and read as uncanny rather than cute
- silhouette differences were too weak; color and size carried most of the distinction
- Tiny / Plump / Spore / Great could not be identified quickly at battle-camera scale

V2 is a full redesign, not a polish pass.

## V2 non-negotiable rules

1. **Eyes are bead-scale, not anime-scale.**
   - eye height target is roughly 6–10% of visible body height, not 25–35%
   - no giant glossy black ovals
   - highlight is optional and tiny

2. **Species differentiation is silhouette-first.**
   A grayscale thumbnail must distinguish all four forms without relying on color.

3. **Each form owns a different vertical/horizontal proportion.**
   - Tiny: button / bean — compact and round
   - Plump: dumpling / bolster — very wide and low
   - Spore: bell / lantern — tall and narrow
   - Great: layered reishi / shelf — broad layered crown and massive body

4. **Face placement stays low and quiet.**
   Small face on a large soft mass reads cuter than a face dominating the model.

5. **Materials stay soft and matte.**
   Avoid wet-plastic caps. Use moderate roughness and restrained coat.

6. **One visual hook per species.**
   - Tiny: simple round button cap + tiny feet
   - Plump: wide dumpling body + sleepy bead eyes
   - Spore: tall bell cap + paired spore bulbs
   - Great: layered shelf cap + two baby mushroom sprouts

7. **No direct IP reproduction.**
   Reference works are used only for proportion, readability, and cute-character design principles.

## Acceptance check

At the production battle camera and in a 128 px thumbnail:

- all four forms must be distinguishable in grayscale
- eyes must not be the first thing the viewer notices
- Tiny must read smallest and lightest
- Plump must read heavy before color is visible
- Spore must read ranged / unusual from silhouette alone
- Great must read boss-class without simply scaling Tiny up

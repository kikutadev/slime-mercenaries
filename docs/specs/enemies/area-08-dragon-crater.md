# Area 8 — Dragon Crater

Status: Approved content specification; implementation may be pending
Date: 2026-09-19

## Identity

Final areaは「可愛い」と「強そう」を両立する。Dragonはlong neck / realistic reptileではなく、large head / short body / small readable wings / one strong star-or-fire hookで設計する。

## Roster

### たまごドラゴン

Role: basic ranged/melee hybrid. Silhouette: round hatchling + eggshell ring around lower body.

- Idle: shell/body tiny wobble
- Move: shell hop
- Attack: inhale → cheek/body inflate → small fireball → recoil / sit
- Hit: shell shakes after body
- Defeat: body ducks into shell, × eyes peek out

### こつばさ竜

Role: mobility melee. Silhouette: large head + comically tiny wings.

- Idle: repeated failed tiny flap
- Move: three quick flaps → brief hover → landing
- Attack: wing charge → one successful short flight → dive bump
- Hit: wings fold
- Defeat: tiny wings flap once, then body sits

### 星くいトカゲ

Role: bruiser. Silhouette: compact lizard + one oversized star/crystal on back.

- Idle: back star slow glow
- Move: low crawl/hop, star lag
- Attack: star goes dark → light travels into body → charged dash
- Hit: star glow flickers without physical break
- Defeat: glow drains, body curls around star

### りゅうせいヒナ

Role: high-tier caster. Silhouette: round hatchling + two small orbiting star motes.

- Idle: two motes orbit slowly
- Move: short float/hop
- Attack: complete still → motes rise → merge → hold → small meteor launch
- Hit: orbit breaks then reforms
- Defeat: motes descend and dim beside body

## Final Boss — 星喰らい竜

Silhouette: enormous head, short body, large readable wings, bounded horns/tail。Realistic adult dragonにはしない。

Attack A — Star Charge: crouch → wings fully close → star light pulls inward → 0.15s hold → wings burst open → charge → delayed tail/wind after-effect。

Attack B — Meteor Drop: jump → leaves camera frame briefly → ground shadow expands → impact → delayed star fragments/ring → long recovery。

Defeat: final breath → glow extinguishes → wings droop → sits down → oversized head drops softly to ground → × / sleepy defeat expression。

## Stage structure

1. たまごドラゴン
2. こつばさ竜
3. 星くいトカゲ
4. りゅうせいヒナ + all-family mix
5. 全4種 → 星喰らい竜

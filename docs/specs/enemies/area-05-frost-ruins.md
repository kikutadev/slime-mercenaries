# Area 5 — Frost Ruins

Status: Approved content specification; implementation may be pending
Date: 2026-09-19

## Identity

Snow mass + restrained crystal/ice accent。Snow bodyはsoftにし、氷の形状は少数の大きなパーツへ整理する。

## Roster

### ゆきころ

Role: basic roller. Silhouette: snowball + one small ice nub.

- Idle: slight settle / snow breathing
- Move: roll with small squash on contact
- Attack: rolling momentum builds → bump → returns to baseline
- Hit: dents then rounds back
- Defeat: slow half-roll, × eyes

### こおりムシ

Role: ranged. Silhouette: low dumpling + exactly three broad ice spikes.

- Idle: spikes breathe/tilt slightly
- Move: short low slide
- Attack: three spikes lean backward → front snap → one compact ice projectile
- Hit: spikes lag behind body recoil
- Defeat: body sits, spikes droop outward


### マフラー雪だるま

Role: mobile melee. Silhouette: one round snow mass + oversized short scarf。Two-snowball human shapeは避ける。

- Idle: scarf slow sway
- Move: small hop, scarf delayed
- Attack: scarf streams backward first → body dash → scarf overshoot
- Hit: scarf wraps partly around body
- Defeat: body sinks into snow, scarf lands last

### つららランタン

Role: caster. Silhouette: compact floating lantern / ice-drop.

- Idle: tiny hover
- Move: glide
- Attack: internal light cyan → white → thin ice ray
- Hit: light flickers, body rotates
- Defeat: light fades, soft snow landing

## Boss — 雪像の番人

Silhouette: two large rounded snow masses + broad ice crest。Humanoid limbsは付けない。

Attack: upper mass rotates first → lower mass follows with delay → full spin → ice wake → long inertial settle。

Secondary: crest glows → snow pulse → delayed bounded icicle landing。

Defeat: top mass drops into snow with a soft ぽすっ。× eyes remain readable。

## Stage structure

1. ゆきころ
2. こおりムシ
3. マフラー雪だるま + ranged mix
4. つららランタン + all four
5. 全4種 → 雪像の番人

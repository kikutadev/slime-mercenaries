# Evolution & Roster

Status: Current
Date: 2026-09-15

## 1. Roster model

Launch roster is fixed at 30 discoverable slime types. The tree is deliberately broad enough to create collection curiosity while remaining readable from six first-job families.

A player does not reroll individual slimes. Plain Slime population is promoted into a type; the type then grows as a squad.

## 4. Rare mutation 5 types

Rare mutations are horizontally special. They are not universally stronger than every Tier 3 type; they create unusual composition questions and strong collection identity.

| # | Mutation | Eligible origin | Role | Surprise path | Deterministic path | Signature |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | King Slime | any Tier 3 | team aura | very rare Royal Crown event after boss chest | 100 Crown Fragments | periodically buffs all deployed squads |
| 27 | Golden Slime | any Tier 2+ | economy/support | rare Golden Jelly mutation on chest open | 100 Gold Gel Fragments | attacks spill bonus Gold; modest combat stats |
| 28 | Dragon Slime | Sword/Shield/Bow/Gun Tier 3 | elite bruiser | Dragon Core can mutate eligible squad | guaranteed Dragon Core from late-world milestone | leap + cone breath |
| 29 | Prism Slime | Wand Tier 3 or Storm Archer | elemental burst | Prism Core from rainbow chest | 100 Prism Shards | cycles fire/frost/lightning, changing VFX and effect |
| 30 | Mimic Slime | any type; separate capture | trick DPS | rare chest reveals friendly mimic candidate | defeat 10 hostile mimics + craft Mimic Heart | bites enemies, then copies last chest weapon effect briefly |

The first surprise mutation should be allowed to occur earlier than the deterministic completion path, but no codex entry should require indefinite luck.

## 5. Promotion rules

### Tier 0 -> Tier 1

Requirements:

- one available Plain Slime population
- one Job Gear family unlock
- small Gold cost

The first Sword and Bow job gears are tutorial-guaranteed. Other families become deterministically craftable as areas unlock even if random equipment drops are unfavorable.

### Tier 1 -> Tier 2

Requirements:

- type level 10
- mastery threshold
- common promotion material
- Gold

Tier 2 is an upgrade of the same branch, not a random result.

### Tier 2 -> Tier 3

Requirements:

- type level 30
- mastery threshold
- branch crest from relevant area/boss
- player selects one of two specializations

Choosing one does not permanently lock the other. The second specialization is discovered by promoting additional population of the same Tier-2 family later.

## 6. Type level, mastery, population

These three concepts must remain separate.

### Type level

Gold-spend growth. Primarily increases combat stats. This is the frequent upgrade button.

### Mastery

Earned by using the type in combat and through duplicate branch equipment. Unlocks promotions and passive milestones. It should not require manually farming a specific weak stage.

### Population

Determines how many bodies the squad visibly fields, up to five. Population growth is intentionally powerful visually.

Suggested milestones:

| Population progress | Visible bodies | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| discovered | 1 | identity |
| squad milestone 1 | 2 | immediately more lively |
| squad milestone 2 | 3 | midgame density |
| squad milestone 3 | 4 | strong army feel |
| squad milestone 4 | 5 | endgame visual cap |

Additional duplicate population beyond five visible bodies can improve squad stats without adding more render entities.

## 7. Role interactions

The six branches should create understandable composition differences without hard rock-paper-scissors requirements.

- Sword: reliable frontline damage
- Shield: enemy attention and survival
- Bow: safe physical range
- Wand: area magic and control
- Dagger: mobility / execute / backline access
- Gun: sustained or explosive ranged damage

Tier 3 choices sharpen behavior:

- Blademaster vs Berserker = precision speed vs risk burst
- Paladin vs Fortress = team sustain vs self durability
- Sniper vs Storm Archer = boss focus vs wave clear
- Archmage vs Frost Mage = damage vs control
- Ninja vs Assassin = mobility/multi-hit vs execution
- Cannoneer vs Engineer = direct AoE vs deployed utility

No branch should exist only as a numeric recolor.

## 8. Discovery presentation

Codex shows all 30 slots from early game but only reveals:

- discovered types in full
- next reachable nodes as silhouettes plus broad hint
- remote Tier 3 / mutations as silhouettes without exact recipe until their prerequisite is near

When a NEW type is created:

1. battlefield briefly slows, but does not hard-cut to a long scene
2. slime jumps / compresses
3. equipment snaps into silhouette
4. bright jelly flash
5. new job name + one-line role tag
6. player returns directly to battle with the new attack demonstrated

Target duration: roughly 1.5–2.5 seconds for normal jobs; mutation reveal may be 3–4 seconds.

## 9. Balance guardrails

- Tier 3 specialization is a sidegrade in role first, power increase second.
- Mutation does not obsolete the normal tree.
- At least four materially different six-squad compositions should remain viable in normal PvE.
- Tank/support value must be expressed through survival/tempo, not mandatory hidden checks.
- A favorite type should remain usable after its branch is discovered; collection growth should not force replacing every old visual immediately.
